# Comedy engines for short ads

Each engine turns a true pain into a joke. Pick the engine, then the situation. These are patterns, not copies of real
ads: never reuse a real campaign's characters, lines or look.

| Engine | How it works | Shape for "cheaper, no subscription" |
|---|---|---|
| Literal | A metaphor becomes a thing. "The subscription eats your money" → a creature that eats cash | A furry monster gulps $49 and spits out four tiny film reels |
| Side-by-side | Same thing, one detail differs; the viewer does the maths | Two friends, the same video; one paid four times more |
| Escalation | A small problem grows each beat until absurd | The plan renews, then renews twice, then moves in and eats breakfast |
| Deadpan | A far too calm (or too serious) reaction | A movie-trailer voice for cancelling a subscription |
| Role reversal | The weak one wins | A kid with pocket money makes what the "pro" paid a fortune for |
| Rule of three | Two normal items, the third breaks the pattern | "I pay for music. I pay for films. I pay for an app I opened twice." |
| Callback | Plant early, pay off at the end | The monster's suitcase seen in beat 1; it packs it in the last beat |
| Wrong guess | A wrong conclusion, the truth is better | "Two-fifty… a month?" "Per video." |

## What a child gets

Faces (surprise, jealousy, smugness, shame), fairness ("he paid more for the same thing!"), money and objects you can
see, animals and machines that act like people.

## What loses a child, and most adults

"API", "credits", "tokens", "tiers" or "pay as you go" without a picture of them; two jokes at once; irony that only
works if you know the industry.
